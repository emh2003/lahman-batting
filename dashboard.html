Notes

How the numbers are computed. Rates are computed from summed totals, never by averaging each row's rate. AVG = H / AB. OBP = (H + BB + HBP) / (AB + BB + HBP + SF). SLG = TB / AB. OPS = OBP + SLG. HR per 600 PA = HR / PA × 600. Strikeout rate = SO / PA, counting only rows where strikeouts were recorded. A blank HBP or SF counts as 0. Rankings (the group bars and the top-10 list) only include groups and players with at least the minimum plate appearances you set.
